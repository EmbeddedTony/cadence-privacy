# Cadence Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Cadence: AMV & Video Editor is an iPhone and iPad editing app published by [EmbeddedTony](https://github.com/EmbeddedTony). This policy explains the current app's data practices.

## Your edits and media

Cadence copies the media you choose into its app storage to save and render your projects. Project names, clips, images, text, audio, waveform analysis, markers, masks, effects and export files are processed on your device. We do not operate a Cadence server that receives your media or projects. Your device's Apple-managed backups may include app data if you enable backups.

Photos import uses Apple's picker for the items you select. Files import accesses the files you choose, which may come from a storage provider. Exporting and sharing use Apple's system interfaces; the destination you choose handles the shared file under its own policies.

## Assisted effects

When available and enabled, Apple Intelligence suggests an editable effect recipe on your device through Apple's Foundation Models framework. Cadence does not upload your prompts or footage to a developer-operated AI service. Devices without the system model use a local keyword-based recipe search. The current assistant does not generate footage or upload media for analysis.

## Purchases

Apple's App Store processes subscriptions and the one-time Studio purchase. Cadence uses StoreKit to check verified purchase entitlements on your device and restore access. We do not receive your payment-card details and do not run a separate purchase-account database. Apple processes purchase and account information under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Subscription management and refund requests are handled through Apple.

## Analytics and advertising

The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Apple may provide the developer with App Store aggregate metrics and crash diagnostics according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies. Cadence may write operational diagnostic logs on your device; we do not operate a service that automatically uploads those logs.

## Retention and your choices

Your project files stay in the app's local storage until removed. To remove Cadence's local app data, use Delete App in your device's settings; offloading the app may retain its data. Copies in device backups, exports or destinations you shared to must be removed separately. You can manage Photos permissions, backups and diagnostic sharing in your device's settings. No Cadence account is required to edit or export.

## This website

GitHub hosts this policy. Visiting it sends normal connection information to GitHub, which handles that information under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). This page loads no third-party scripts, advertisements or analytics.

## Questions and changes

For privacy questions, [open an issue in the public Cadence privacy repository](https://github.com/EmbeddedTony/cadence-privacy/issues). Issues are public: do not include private footage, account details, payment information or other sensitive information. For Apple Account, billing or refund questions, contact Apple Support.

Material changes to the app's data practices will be reflected in an updated policy and effective date here.
