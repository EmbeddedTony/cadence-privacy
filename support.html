Help for Cadence: AMV & Video Editor.

Start your first edit

Create a project, choose vertical or horizontal, and use Add media to import video from Photos or Files. Video sound becomes its own linked audio clip. Tap a clip to select; hold it for quick actions, or drag its body and edges to move and trim. Pinch the timeline to zoom.

Effects and sound

Select a video, image or text layer and open Effects & animation. Preview a recipe, adjust timing and motion, then Apply. Cancel preserves your work. Place landing lets you choose an impact point in the preview. Select audio to suggest sound accents; snapping helps align cuts with markers.

Watermarks and purchases

Free exports include a low-opacity Cadence pattern across the full video frame. The separate No Watermark one-time purchase removes it from future exports permanently without unlocking advanced Pro tools. Active Pro subscriptions remove it while active. If Pro ends, future exports regain the watermark unless you own No Watermark or Studio. Previously exported videos never change. Studio includes local Pro tools and permanent watermark removal.

Restore or manage a purchase

Open Cadence Pro plans and choose Restore purchases using the Apple Account that bought it. Manage subscriptions opens Apple's settings. A one-time purchase does not cancel an existing subscription. Apple handles payment and refunds; Apple billing support can help with charges.

Import or export trouble

Use the native Photos picker or Files. Cloud media must finish downloading. Keep enough free device storage for both owned source copies and the exported video. Missing media errors preserve the project reference. Export shows watermark status before rendering; cancel returns to editing.

Contact support

Report a problem or ask a question. Include your device model, OS version, app version and steps to reproduce. GitHub issues are public: do not post private footage, payment details, Apple Account information or other sensitive data.
